title: Add a creator
description: Invite Oreo and add your first creator.
---

Oreo posts in your server when a creator goes live or uploads. Setup takes about a minute.

1. [Invite Oreo](/invite) to your server. Keep every permission ticked.
2. Open the [dashboard](/dashboard) and log in with Discord.
3. Pick your server. Servers without Oreo are under **Add Oreo**.

![Your servers](/guide-assets/add-servers.png)

4. Go to **Creators** and click **Add creator**.
5. Pick the platform. Paste the channel link or type the handle. Oreo shows the channel so you can check it.
6. Pick the **Discord channel** to post in. Add **Ping roles** if you want them.
7. For YouTube, choose what to announce: livestreams, videos, premieres, shorts. **Members-only posts** are off by default.
8. Click **Add**.

![Adding a creator](/guide-assets/add-dialog.png)

That's it. The creator may show **Connecting** for a moment while the platform confirms.

![Your creators](/guide-assets/add-creators.png)

## Links Oreo understands

| Platform | Paste any of these |
| --- | --- |
| YouTube | `youtube.com/@oreohq`, `youtube.com/channel/UC…`, `youtube.com/c/name`, `youtube.com/user/name`, `@oreohq` or the channel ID |
| Twitch | `twitch.tv/oreohq` or the username |
| Kick | `kick.com/oreohq` or the username |

<Callout title="Limits">
<PremiumOnly>Free servers get one creator per platform. [Premium](/guide/premium) servers get 25.</PremiumOnly>
<PremiumOff>Each server can have one creator per platform.</PremiumOff>
</Callout>

**Next:** [set up the creator](/guide/customize) or [send a test](/guide/testnotifications).

## In Discord

Run **/settings** and press **Quick Setup**, or open a platform and press **Add Creator**. See [Set up in Discord](/guide/quick-setup). The dashboard and **/settings** change the same settings.
