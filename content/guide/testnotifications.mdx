title: Send a test
description: See a notification in your server now.
---

A test posts a sample notification in the creator's channel. Use it to check the channel, the message and Oreo's permissions.

1. Open the creator in the [dashboard](/dashboard).
2. Save your changes. Tests use the saved settings.
3. Under the live preview, click **Send test notification**.
4. Click **Open in Discord** to see it.

![A test notification](/guide-assets/test-sent.png)

<Callout>
Tests never ping anyone. The message shows the roles, but nobody is notified.
</Callout>

## If it fails

The dashboard says why. It's usually a missing permission.

- Run **/settings** in Discord and open the creator. It shows what Oreo is missing in that channel.
- Real notifications late? Check [Oreo's status](/status).

## In Discord

Run **/settings**, pick the creator and press **Send test**.
