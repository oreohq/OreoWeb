title: Bot profile

---

Premium servers can give Oreo their own look. It only changes Oreo in your server.

1. Open your server in the [dashboard](/dashboard) and go to **Bot profile**.
2. Change what you want:
   - **Name**: up to 32 characters.
   - **Bio**: up to 190 characters.
   - **Picture**: PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP, up to 2 MB.
   - **Banner**: PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP, up to 4 MB. About 3:1 looks best.
3. Click **Save**.

![Bot profile](/guide-assets/bot-profile.png)

## Good to know

- You can save up to 8 times an hour.
- If premium runs out, Oreo goes back to its normal look. Your profile is kept and comes back with premium.
- **Delete profile** resets everything and erases the saved copy.

<Callout type="warn">
Oreo needs the **Change Nickname** permission to use its new name.
</Callout>
