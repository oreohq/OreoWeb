Each creator has its own settings. Open one from **Creators** in the [dashboard](/dashboard).

The header shows the essentials at a glance: on or paused, the channel, what gets announced and who gets pinged. Click any of them to jump to that setting.

## General

- **Post notifications** turns the creator on or off. Settings are kept while it's off.
- **Channel** is where notifications go, like #oreo-live. Any text or announcement channel that you and Oreo can post in. Picking the first channel turns the creator on.
- **Announce** picks the kinds of posts: livestreams, videos, premieres, shorts and members-only posts. Members-only is off by default. This is for YouTube only. Twitch and Kick announce livestreams.
- **Publish in announcement channels** also sends each post to servers that follow the channel.

**Post each kind in its own channel** is for YouTube. Pick a channel for livestreams, videos, premieres or shorts, and leave the rest on **Main channel**. Members-only posts go to the channel of what they are: a stream, premiere, short or video.

## The other sections

Switch between them with the tabs at the top. A red dot marks a tab with something to fix.

- **Pings & roles**: see [Ping roles](/guide/pingrole).
- **Message**: see [Write the message](/guide/placeholders).
- **Embed** and **Buttons**: see [Style the embed](/guide/embeds).
- **Stream end**: update the post, delete it, or keep it. See [When the stream ends](/guide/stream-end).
- **Danger zone**: remove the creator from this server.

![Buttons](/guide-assets/customize-more.png)

## Saving

The live preview updates as you type. Click **Save changes**, or press **Ctrl + S** (**⌘ S** on a Mac).

Leave without saving and the dashboard asks first. Unsaved changes survive going back and forward.

<Callout>
To pause a creator quickly, use the switch next to its name in **Creators**.
</Callout>

## In Discord

Run **/settings**, pick the creator, then choose a section.
