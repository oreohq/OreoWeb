---
title: Ping roles
description: Ping the right people, and let members opt in.
---

Each creator can ping its own roles. Members can opt in themselves, and the creator can get a live role.

## Ping roles

1. Open the creator in the [dashboard](/dashboard) and go to **Pings & roles**.
2. Add up to 5 **Ping roles**, like @Stream Pings. Every kind of post pings them.
3. Keep `{role.pingrole}` in the message. Oreo's default messages already have it.
4. Click **Save changes**.

![Pings and roles](/guide-assets/pingrole-section.png)

### Different roles per kind of post

Want one role for streams and another for videos?

1. Go to **Message** and pick the kind of post.
2. Choose **Specific roles** and pick its roles. Or choose **No ping**.
3. **Default roles** goes back to the main list.

## Let members opt in

Turn on the **"Notify me" button**. Members press it to get the roles that post pings, or pick creators with **/notify**. See [Let members opt in](/guide/notify).

## Live role

Give the creator a role while they're live, so they stand out.

1. Set a **Live role**.
2. Paste the creator's Discord user ID. Turn on Developer Mode, right-click them, then **Copy User ID**. They have to be in your server.

Oreo removes the role when the stream ends, and after 24 hours at the latest.

<Callout type="warn" title="Permissions">
For the "Notify me" button and the live role, you need **Manage Roles**. The role must sit below your highest role and below Oreo's. It can't have moderator permissions.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">
Pinging @everyone, @here or a non-mentionable role needs **Mention @everyone, @here and All Roles**, for you and for Oreo.
</Callout>

## In Discord

Run **/settings**, pick the creator, then **Pings** or **Live role**.
