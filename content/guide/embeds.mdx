The embed is the card under the message. Restyle any part of it, or turn it off.

1. Open the creator in the [dashboard](/dashboard) and go to **Embed**.
2. **Show the embed** turns it on or off. Off means only the message is posted.
3. Pick a **Colour**: the platform's colour (YouTube red, Twitch purple, Kick green), a swatch, or any colour from the picker.
4. Edit the **Author**, **Title**, **Description** and **Footer**. [Placeholders](/guide/placeholders) work in all of them.
5. Pick the pictures. Each can be none, the creator's avatar, the video thumbnail or an image link.
6. Turn on the extras you want.
7. Check the live preview, then click **Save changes**.

![Styling the embed](/guide-assets/embeds-section.png)

**Reset** puts the embed back to Oreo's default.

## What each part is

| Part | Where it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Author icon** | Small round picture beside the author |
| **Thumbnail** | Top right corner |
| **Image** | Large picture under the text |
| **Link the title** | Title opens the video, or the recording after a stream |
| **Timestamp** | When it went up, in each member's timezone |
| **Show category** | Adds a "Playing" or "Category" field |

### After a stream

Ended streams always turn grey. The footer says "Live now", then "Stream ended".

## Buttons

The **Watch button** adds "Watch Stream" or "Watch Video" under each notification.

The **"Notify me"** button lives in **Pings & roles**. See [Ping roles](/guide/pingrole).

## In Discord

Run **/settings**, pick the creator and choose **Embed**. The watch button is under **More**.
