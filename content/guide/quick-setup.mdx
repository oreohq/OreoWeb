Everything in the dashboard is also in Discord. Run **/settings** in your server. You need **Manage Server**.

The settings panel only shows for you. Run `/settings visibility:Public` to let others watch, for example while helping someone set up.

{/* screenshot needed: settings-home.png (The /settings home page) */}

## Quick Setup

Press **Quick Setup** on the home page. It takes three steps:

1. **Add a creator.** Pick the platform and paste the channel link or name. A platform is greyed out when its slots are full.
2. **Pick a channel**, like #oreo-live. Text and announcement channels work.
3. **Pick ping roles**, up to 5, like @Stream Pings. Turn the **Notify me** button on or off, then press **Finish**. No pings? Press **Skip Pings**.

{/* screenshot needed: settings-quick-setup.png (Quick Setup) */}

When it's done you get three buttons: **Send Test**, **Their Settings** to fine-tune the creator, and **Add Another**.

## Find your way around

### The home page

The menu opens **YouTube**, **Twitch**, **Kick** or **Counters**. Below it are **Quick Setup**, links to the dashboard and this guide, and your server's plan.

### Platform pages

- A platform page lists its creators, 25 per page. The dot shows their state: green is on, orange is paused, red has no channel, and a warning means Oreo can't post in the channel.
- **Add Creator** asks for the channel link or name.

### A creator's page

- Open a creator for its settings: **Channel**, **Announces**, **Pings**, **Messages**, **Embed** and **More**.
- **Preview** shows the notification only to you, with no pings. **Send Test** posts it in the channel.
- **Remove** deletes the creator. Type `remove` to confirm.

{/* screenshot needed: settings-creator.png (A creator in /settings) */}

### Saving

Every change saves as you make it. The panel closes after 14 minutes without use. Run **/settings** again to reopen it.
