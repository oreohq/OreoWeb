## Your servers

The [dashboard](/dashboard) lists every server you own or have **Manage Server** in.

- **With Oreo**: open one to manage it.
- **Oreo isn't here yet**: click one to invite Oreo there.

![Your servers](/guide-assets/add-servers.png)

## Overview

The first page of a server shows how it's doing:

- **Stats**: creators, counters, and notifications sent and delivered in the last 30 days.
- **Notifications chart**: a day-by-day count for the last 30 days, per platform.
- **Live now**: creators who are live right now.
- **Health**: a checklist of anything that needs fixing.

![Overview](/guide-assets/overview-page.png)

### The health checklist

It looks for:

- Permissions Oreo is missing in the server, with a link to grant them.
- Creators with no channel, or a channel Oreo can't post in.
- Creators that aren't connected to their platform yet.
- Counters that can't be updated.
- Problems on Oreo's side, like the bot being offline or notifications running late.

When everything is fine, it suggests sending a test so you can see exactly what members get.

## Recent activity

**Recent activity** on the Overview lists the latest notifications Oreo posted, newest first. A failed one says why, for example "Missing permissions in #oreo-live". Click a sent one to open it in Discord.

Failures are almost always a permission or a deleted channel. See [Permissions and fixes](/guide/permissions).
