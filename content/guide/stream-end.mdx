---
title: When the stream ends
description: Update, delete or keep the post when a stream ends.
---

A go-live post is only right while the stream is on. Choose what Oreo does with it afterwards. This setting shows when the creator announces livestreams.

1. Open the creator in the [dashboard](/dashboard) and go to **When the stream ends**.
2. Pick one:
   - **Update it** (default): the post turns grey, says how long the stream lasted, and links the recording.
   - **Delete it**: keeps the channel tidy, so only live streams stay listed.
   - **Leave it**: the post stays exactly as it was.
3. Click **Save changes**.

![When the stream ends](/guide-assets/stream-end-section.png)

## What an updated post looks like

- The embed turns grey and the footer changes from "Live now" to "Stream ended".
- A **Streamed for** field shows how long it lasted, for example 2h 14m.
- A line under the message says when it ended.
- The button changes to **Watch the VOD**, or **Visit on Twitch** when there's no recording.
- `{video.link}` points to the recording.

Platforms take a while to publish recordings. Oreo keeps checking for up to 90 minutes and links it as soon as it's ready.

Switch the live preview to **Ended** to see it before it happens.

![The same post after the stream, in the preview's Ended tab](/guide-assets/stream-end-ended.png)

## Good to know

- If a Kick stream restarts within 3 minutes, Oreo updates the same post instead of pinging again.
- In announcement channels, **Publish in announcement channels** also sends each post to servers that follow the channel. Discord allows 10 published posts per channel per hour.

## In Discord

Run **/settings**, pick the creator and open **More**.
