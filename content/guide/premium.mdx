Oreo is free. Premium raises the limits and adds a few extras.

|  | Free | Premium |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Creators | 1 per platform | 25 per platform |
| Counters | 2 | 10 |
| [Bot profile](/guide/bot-profile) | No | Yes |
| Supporter role in the support server | No | Yes |

## How premium works

Premium belongs to **you**, not to a server. You buy it, then give it to the servers you manage.

### Get premium

1. Get premium on Ko-fi. Pick an item from the shop, or join a membership tier. Buying again adds more time.
2. Open **Premium** in the [dashboard](/dashboard).
3. If your Discord account is connected on Ko-fi, the payment shows up by itself. If not, [claim it](#claim-a-payment).

### Claim a payment

Paid on Ko-fi without connecting Discord? Claim the payment so it's added to your account.

1. Open **Premium** in the [dashboard](/dashboard) and click **Claim a payment**.
2. Enter the **Transaction ID** from the receipt Ko-fi emailed you.
3. Enter the **email you paid with**. It's only used to match the payment.
4. Click **Claim**. The premium is now yours to give to a server.

- Claiming adds **every** unclaimed payment made with that email, not just the one you entered.
- Bought time starts counting when you claim it, not when you paid.
- A payment can only belong to one Discord account. A membership that ended before it was claimed can't be claimed.
- If it says no payment matches, check the transaction ID and email, or wait a minute for Ko-fi to send it.

### Give it to a server

On the same page, pick a server you manage and give it premium. The new limits apply straight away.

![Premium](/guide-assets/premium-page.png)

## Good to know

- You can move premium to another server 7 days after giving it.
- Memberships renew with each payment, with a few days of grace if a payment is late.
- If your premium covers fewer servers than you gave it to, the server that got it last pauses first.
- A server that loses premium keeps its creators and counters. It just can't add more past the free limits.

Questions about premium? Ask in the [support server](/support).
